What's Inside
One dashboard. Eight jobs.
WindowNameDescription
①New LaunchesEvery token that just deployed, as it hits the chain. Shows the creator's KOL history, early tx count, and cross-chain activity so you can judge it in the first few minutes. RH+4
②Top 100Top 100 by 24h volume on Robinhood Chain. Full market data, X activity, and a direct link to the chart. RH
③WatchlistTokens you're tracking. Set your own refresh rate per address, watch KOL and smart money flow in, and kick off a deep report any time. all chains
④New ≤3 DaysTop 100 filtered to tokens under 72 hours old. On-chain age is verified — migrated contracts don't sneak in and inflate the list. RH
⑤AI · RobinhoodRuns a scoring pass every 10 minutes. Pulls holder data, trade history, and X signals, then writes a 1–100 score with a plain-English reason for each token in your market-cap range. RH
⑥AI · BSCSame scoring engine as ⑤, running independently on BSC. Its market-cap band and age cutoff are set separately. BSC
⑦AI · BaseSame scoring engine as ⑤, running independently on Base. Its market-cap band and age cutoff are set separately. Base
⑧Deep ReportPaste a contract address, pick a chain. It pulls holders, trades, and X posts and builds an illustrated PDF with a price chart, volume bars, and holder breakdown. Usually done in 3–8 minutes. RHBSCBase
⚙️AdminOne place to tune the whole backend: concurrency, AI model, refresh intervals per window, market-cap bands for each scorer, Telegram alert routing, and a live view of what's currently running.
