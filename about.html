What Monhood is not.

It is a research tool, not a promise. Scores and alerts are inputs for your process—not investment advice or a substitute for due diligence.

Crypto markets move quickly and data can be incomplete, delayed, or wrong. Validate contracts, liquidity, ownership, and risks independently before making any decision.
