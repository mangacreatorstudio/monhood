1. Overview

Monhood ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website at moonhood.fun. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Wallet address. When you connect a wallet (MetaMask or compatible), we record your public wallet address for the purpose of authenticating your session. We never request access to your private keys or the ability to sign transactions on your behalf beyond the one-time authentication challenge.
  • Usage data. We may collect anonymised data about how pages are accessed (page views, referrer, browser type, approximate geographic region) through server logs or analytics tools. This data is aggregated and does not identify individuals.
  • Cookies and local storage. We use session storage and cookies as described in our Cookie Policy.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any payment information unless you voluntarily provide it to us through a contact form or similar mechanism.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your session and provide access to the dashboard.
  • To maintain and improve the security and functionality of the site.
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns and improve the product.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Blockchain Data

Your wallet address is a public identifier on the blockchain. Information associated with your address (transaction history, token balances, on-chain activity) is publicly available and not within our control. We may display publicly available on-chain data as part of the dashboard functionality.

5. Data Retention

Authentication session data is stored in your browser's session storage and is automatically cleared when you close the browser tab. We do not store wallet addresses on our servers beyond what is necessary for temporary session validation.

6. Third-Party Services

Our service relies on third-party infrastructure including GitHub Pages for hosting. These providers have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them. We do not control and are not responsible for the practices of those providers.

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure. You use our service at your own risk, and we encourage you to take appropriate precautions with your wallet and credentials.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Because we store minimal personal data (primarily your public wallet address in session storage, which you control), most requests can be fulfilled by clearing your browser session.
